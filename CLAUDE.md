# The goal is to make visibility of taxes accessible to anyone
Any german speaking or english speaking citizen in Germany or outside Germany
browsing this webpage must be able at a glance in one page to understand, absorb
and explore where the taxes of a German citizen are going. This must be an
intuitive, human, fun and interactive way of discovery. People must leave the
page with a "ah-ah!" feeling.

## The approach is data first
Data first, the we build a front-end around it.

This is built bottom up. First all data and facts about taxes are collected.
Once this is done a rationalization step follows. Taxes as they are are a
complex topic and need to be brought down to simple and graspable concepts.
For example, frame research, findings and numbers in different ways and find for
each topic the neatest and most elegant construct. Review your work with other
independent agents and pieced together to offer a complete and armonious drill
down from start to end.

## Truth and sourcing is of maximum criticality
All the gathered information must be true, verified and must come from a trusted
source. Nothing of what's shown to the user should be contestable. If any
research piece adds fundamental value but the origin is questionable you must
state so (for example with the use of *, notes etc..).

## Your research is part of this repo
Dedicate a folder, rationalized in raw material and reconstructed artifacts for
the means of being served by the page.

## Front end it's not an app
It is an interactive slide. Designed with modern, widespread and fully
customizable plotting, charting, drawing libraries for maximum control, result
and potential to empower the user in understanding things visually, less text,
more visuals. No AI explaination of obvious things. No AI slop text, No AI
flashy headlines. Focus on how to show all the underlying data in the best
possible way. Front-end must be served on github-docs.
