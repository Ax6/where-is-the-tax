availability into a permanent premise.

- name, publisher, URL, and date checked;
- country/accounting scope and source transparency;
- visual form and interaction model;
- how it explains totals, balance, uncertainty, and non-earmarking;
- accessibility and no-JavaScript behavior;
- ideas worth testing, patterns to avoid, and why; and
- whether claims were directly observed or inferred.

Prior art informs experiments; it cannot relax the accounting or comprehension gates in the plan.
