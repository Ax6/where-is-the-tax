## Stable project decisions

- The publication frame is consolidated ESA 2010 general government (S.13).
- Revenue and expenditure are shown side by side but are not presented as earmarked flows.
- Cash-basis national figures cannot decompose ESA accrual parents.
- A generic landing page is insufficient provenance; record extraction and observation
  coordinates separately.
- Source rounding determines reconciliation bounds. Fixed percentage tolerances are forbidden.
- Missing and unavailable observations are never converted to zero.
- Stable IDs do not prove longitudinal comparability when classifications or coverage change.

These are product-contract decisions documented in `PLAN.md`; external accounting explanations
still need authoritative evidence during research.
